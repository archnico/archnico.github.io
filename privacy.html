Privacy policy — Astronave rclone

Astronave rclone is a personal-use integration operated by the owner of the Google accounts it connects to.

Google user data

The integration accesses Google Drive files and metadata only after the account owner grants OAuth authorization. Access is used solely to browse, synchronize, back up, and restore data between Google Drive and storage controlled by the same owner.

Storage and sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Data copied from Google Drive is stored only on systems and storage destinations controlled by the account owner. OAuth credentials are stored on those systems for the purpose of maintaining authorized access.

Retention and revocation

Locally synchronized copies are retained according to the owner’s backup and retention settings. Google access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings; local credentials can then be removed by the owner.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing and maintaining this personal synchronization and backup functionality.
